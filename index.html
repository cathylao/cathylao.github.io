Hi there Welcome to my Homepage!

I am a researcher at ByteDance Seed, working on video generation and multimodal foundation models. On Seedance 1.5 Pro, Seedance 2.0, and Seedance 2.5, I mainly work on post-training, with a focus on reinforcement learning algorithms for video generation models.

I previously worked in ByteDance's Intelligent Creation team, and interned at SenseTime Research on efficient detection and diffusion models.

I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Tsinghua University and my B.E. in Automation from Beihang University.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in collaboration.
